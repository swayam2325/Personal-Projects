How powerups work

Powerups are earned, never bought. Each rung below pays out once per game, the first time a merge creates that tile. Tiles made by a powerup don't count — otherwise Upgrade would pay for its own next use.

  • 32 ↶ Undo
  • 64 🔨 Hammer
  • 128 ↶ Undo
  • 256 ⇄ Swap
  • 512 ⬆ Upgrade
  • 1024 ★ Wildcard
  • 2048 one of everything — and again at every doubling above

A ★ wildcard merges with whatever it slides into, becoming double that tile's value. Two wildcards meeting settle for a 4.
